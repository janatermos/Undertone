
# Undertone 🎵

**A private songwriting and poetry workspace.**

Undertone is a personal creative space designed to help writers organize their poems, lyrics, and musical ideas.

The goal is to build a workspace where writing stays yours, every version has its history, and your words remain connected to the music that inspired them.

## ✨ Planned Features

- **Writing Workspace:** Create and organize poems and songs.
- **Version History:** Preserve previous versions with their original creation and modification dates.
- **Audio Attachments:** Attach musical recordings and soundtracks directly to individual writing projects.
- **AI Writing Assistant:** Get contextual assistance while maintaining ownership of your original writing.
- **Organization:** Keep creative projects organized and easy to navigate.
